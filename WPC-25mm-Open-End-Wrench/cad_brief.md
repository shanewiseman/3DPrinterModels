# CAD brief

- Model: WPC 25 mm open-end wrench, three labeled color bodies for multi-material printing.
- Task type: parametric CAD modification shortening the parallel jaw section while preserving the established handle, throat, fit, edge treatment, color split, and logo.
- Units: millimeters.
- Coordinate convention: wrench profile lies in XY; extrusion is +Z; the nominal bolt center is at XY origin; jaw opens toward +X; handle extends toward -X.
- Functional size: nominal 25 mm across-flats hex fastener; jaw opening is 25.4 mm to provide 0.2 mm clearance per flat for a first FDM print. The straight parallel jaw section is 30.0 mm from the throat tangent at X=-2.0 mm to the mouth at X=28.0 mm; including the rounded throat, total inlet depth is 42.7 mm.
- Profile depth: 25.0 mm in Z.
- Handle length: 101.6 mm (4.000 in) from the rear head/neck datum at X=-27.0 mm to the physical handle end at X=-128.6 mm.
- Handle intent: smooth convex palm bulge to 36 mm overall width, rounded end, and one shallow continuous R210 finger contour along the lower edge. The contour peak is located at the exact midpoint of the handle-only span, halfway between the physical handle end at X=-128.6 mm and the rear head datum at X=-27.0 mm: X=-77.8 mm.
- Head/neck intent: 27 mm head radius, reinforced 28 mm minimum neck width, tangent convex transitions between the head, neck, grip, and palm stations, a semicircular jaw throat to avoid square stress risers, and jaw tips moved rearward to provide the requested 30 mm straight section.
- Edge treatment: consistent 2 mm fillets on every exposed sharp external edge. Preserve the complete bolt-entry interface as unselected geometry: both 25.4 mm parallel jaw flats, the semicircular throat, and the end edges on both jaw-tip faces.
- Marking: 25 mm photo-inspired circular logo on the top face, uniformly scaled from the original artwork geometry and constructed with concentric rings, curved `WISEMAN PRECISION` and `CARTRIDGES` text, separator dots, stacked W/PC monogram, `ACTON, MA`, and `EST. 2026` details. The logo is centered at X=-49.7 mm, Y=0 in the widest usable flat section of the structural neck search region (X=-60..-27 mm). Its 12.5 mm outer radius retains at least 1.0 mm design clearance from the finished top-face perimeter and R2 edge fillets. The logo remains a flush 0.8 mm-deep secondary-color inlay, not an open engraving.
- Color split: the complete wrench volume from Z=0 through Z=5 mm is a labeled secondary-color body; the remaining body from Z=5 through Z=25 mm is primary color except for the top logo pocket/inlay.
- Manufacturing assumption: conceptual/light-duty FDM tool; print strength depends on material, layer orientation, walls/infill, temperature, and fastener torque. No FEA, torque rating, or safety certification is claimed.
