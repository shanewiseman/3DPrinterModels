FIT-REVIEW PROTOTYPE — VERIFY PHYSICAL INTERFACES BEFORE LOADING OR INSTALLATION

Prepared from the validated parametric STEP assembly. Units: millimeters. Global front is the X2D front-door side; left and right are viewed while facing the printer.

Read before assembly

Safety, scope, and orientation

This is not a production-certified or load-rated mount. The X2D top rim, glass bearing surface, side contact zones, door envelope, AMS feet, and AMS underside clearances still use provisional geometry. Do not place loaded AMS units on the mount until those interfaces are measured and the structure is proof-tested away from the printer.
DirectionMeaning in this guide
FrontThe side with the X2D front door. In model coordinates this is negative Y.
Left / rightViewed while standing in front of and facing the printer.
Shelf rootThe inboard shelf edge beside the printer. The shelf extends outward from this edge.
Top datumZ=0: shelf support surface, provisional X2D top surface, and AMS foot bottoms.

Required physical checks

  • Measure the real AMS foot centers, foot size, foot height, and underside clearance.
  • Confirm the X2D rim/glass load path and whether any printed tie may contact the glass.
  • Confirm all four side-pad bearing zones avoid seams, vents, doors, and removable panels.
  • Cycle the front door through its full physical swing with the unloaded mount dry-fitted.
  • Determine M4 screw lengths from printed coupons and actual hardware; lengths are not finalized in the CAD.
No aluminum or metal structural beams are used. M4 screws and standard hex nuts remain purchased assembly hardware. Add a separate retention tether for each AMS before operational use.

Use hand tools only during fit-up. Do not clamp against the glass or torque printed ASA interfaces until the physical contact geometry is approved.

Preparation

  • Print brackets on their broad truss faces, ties and shelves flat, and pads on their largest faces—the individual STEP files are already saved this way.
  • Deburr holes and pocket entrances without enlarging load-bearing walls.
  • Test one M4 nut trap, one tie button-head recess, one tie overlap, and one center-boss joint before full-size printing.
  • Keep left/right and front/rear brackets labeled; their side-entry nut-pocket orientations differ.
  • Keep tie halves labeled: left halves carry center screw-head recesses; right halves carry center nut pockets.
Assembly order: build the left and right shelf/bracket modules on a protected bench, dry-fit the four pads and modules to verified printer contact zones, then install the front and rear ties while the AMS units are removed.
1

Stage the left shelf

left_shelf.step
SHELFNO HARDWARE YET

Action

  1. Place the shelf top face upward on padded blocks so the underside and bracket lands remain accessible.
  2. Identify the inboard root edge that will sit beside the left printer side and the outboard edge that will extend 25.4 mm beyond the left AMS body.
  3. Confirm the four-hole rows for the front and rear brackets and the two staggered three-hole tie-overlap patterns are clear.
Check: The part lies flat without rocking. Do not interchange it with the right shelf; the tie-hole patterns are mirrored.
2

Attach the left front bracket

left_front_bracket.step
BRACKET4× M4 + 4 NUTS

Action

  1. Load four M4 nuts into the side-entry pockets in the bracket’s 25.4 mm top chord.
  2. Hold the top chord beneath the left shelf’s front row, with the vertical chord at the shelf root beside the future printer side.
  3. Insert four button-head screws through the plain shelf holes and start every nut before tightening; the heads remain proud of the shelf top.
  4. Snug in an alternating pattern; leave final torque for the unloaded fit check.
Check: The front bracket is square to the shelf and the broad truss face points front/rear—not inward toward the printer.
3

Attach the left rear bracket

left_rear_bracket.step
BRACKET4× M4 + 4 NUTS

Action

  1. Load four nuts into the rear bracket’s side-entry top-chord pockets.
  2. Align the bracket beneath the left shelf’s rear four-hole row with its vertical chord at the root.
  3. Insert four button-head screws through the plain shelf holes, engage all nuts, and snug in an alternating pattern; the heads remain proud.
  4. Compare front and rear bracket spacing and parallelism.
Check: Both bracket vertical chords share the same left-side contact plane and neither bracket is twisted.
4

Dry-fit the left front side pad

left_front_side_pad.step
SIDE PADNO MODELED FASTENER

Action

  1. Place the 3 mm pad between the left front bracket’s vertical chord and the verified left-front printer bearing zone.
  2. Align its 120 mm dimension vertically and its 30 mm dimension front-to-back in the installed orientation.
  3. Use only a temporary removable locator during dry fit if required.
Provisional interface: The CAD defines no permanent pad fastener. Do not glue or clamp this pad until the physical side geometry and service-panel clearance are verified.
5

Dry-fit the left rear side pad

left_rear_side_pad.step
SIDE PADNO MODELED FASTENER

Action

  1. Place the pad between the left rear vertical chord and the verified left-rear printer bearing zone.
  2. Keep the pad fully on a structural, non-removable surface and clear of seams, vents, and fasteners.
  3. Confirm the front and rear pads create a common contact plane without forcing the brackets apart.
Check: The unloaded left module sits level at the modeled top datum without pressing on the glass edge.
6

Stage the right shelf

right_shelf.step
SHELFNO HARDWARE YET

Action

  1. Place the right shelf top face upward on padded blocks.
  2. Identify its inboard root edge beside the right printer side and the outboard edge that will extend 25.4 mm beyond the right AMS body.
  3. Confirm all bracket and tie holes are open and that underside nut pockets accept the coupon-tested M4 nuts.
Check: Compare left and right shelves side-by-side: they must be mirrored, not stacked in the same orientation.
7

Attach the right front bracket

right_front_bracket.step
BRACKET4× M4 + 4 NUTS

Action

  1. Load four nuts into the right front bracket’s top-chord pockets.
  2. Align it beneath the right shelf’s front hole row with the vertical chord at the shelf root.
  3. Insert four button-head screws through the plain shelf holes from above, engage all nuts, and snug evenly; the heads remain proud.
Check: The bracket mirrors the left-front bracket and its nut-pocket entries remain accessible for service.
8

Attach the right rear bracket

right_rear_bracket.step
BRACKET4× M4 + 4 NUTS

Action

  1. Load four nuts into the right rear bracket’s top-chord pockets.
  2. Align it beneath the rear row and insert four button-head screws through the plain shelf holes; the heads remain proud.
  3. Snug evenly and compare front/rear bracket parallelism.
Check: Both right-side vertical chords share one contact plane and the shelf remains flat.
9

Dry-fit the right front side pad

right_front_side_pad.step
SIDE PADNO MODELED FASTENER

Action

  1. Place the pad between the right front vertical chord and the verified right-front printer bearing zone.
  2. Align it vertically, keep it clear of seams and removable panels, and use only a temporary locator if needed.
Do not preload the panel. The pad spreads contact; it is not permission to clamp an unverified sheet-metal or glass interface.
10

Dry-fit the right rear side pad

right_rear_side_pad.step
SIDE PADNO MODELED FASTENER

Action

  1. Place the fourth pad at the verified right-rear bearing zone.
  2. Confirm the two right pads form one plane and the right shelf matches the left shelf’s support height.
  3. With a helper and no AMS load, position both shelf/bracket modules on the printer-side dry-fit locations.
Transition check: Both shelves must be level at Z=0, the front door must cycle freely, and neither module may contact glass edges or service panels before ties are added.
11

Install the front tie — left half

front_tie_left_half.step
LOW TIE3× M4 + 3 NUTS

Action

  1. Place the half at the front tie station, modeled at Y=-70 mm, with its 50 mm solid land overlapping the left shelf top.
  2. Keep the 3 mm truss span toward the printer center and the 25.4 mm boss at the center gap.
  3. Insert three vertical screws through the recessed tie holes; capture three nuts in the shelf underside pockets and snug.
Check: The tie rests on the approved top/rim datum, not an unverified glass surface, and remains below the provisional AMS body underside.
12

Install the front tie — right half

front_tie_right_half.step
LOW TIE6× M4 + 6 NUTS

Action

  1. Fasten the right 50 mm overlap to the right shelf using three recessed vertical screws and three underside nuts.
  2. Bring the two center bosses together without overlap or force.
  3. Insert three transverse M4 screws from the left boss head recesses into three nuts captured in the right boss.
  4. Snug the six fasteners involved in this step evenly.
Check: The assembled front tie is straight, its boss stays entirely inside the 25.4 mm AMS gap, and the full physical door sweep remains unobstructed.
13

Install the rear tie — left half

rear_tie_left_half.step
LOW TIE3× M4 + 3 NUTS

Action

  1. Place the left half at the rear tie station, modeled at Y=+70 mm.
  2. Overlap its solid land 50 mm onto the left shelf and keep the boss toward the center gap.
  3. Install three recessed vertical screws and three shelf-underside nuts; leave the center joint accessible.
Check: The rear tie clears the measured AMS underside, vents, PTFE routes, rear modules, and glass-service path.
14

Install the rear tie — right half

rear_tie_right_half.step
LOW TIE6× M4 + 6 NUTS

Action

  1. Fasten the right overlap with three vertical screws and three underside nuts.
  2. Align the rear center bosses and insert three transverse screws from left to right into the right-half nut pockets.
  3. Snug the rear joint, then recheck all front/rear tie and bracket fasteners by hand.
Check: Both ties are parallel, the shelf tops remain coplanar, and the assembly is stable without forcing the printer sides or top.
Final unloaded fit check

Verify geometry before placing either AMS

  • Centered pair: AMS bodies face forward, centers are X=±198.7 mm, and the body-to-body gap is 25.4 mm.
  • Outer margin: AMS outer faces are X=±384.7 mm; shelf edges are X=±410.1 mm—exactly 25.4 mm beyond each AMS.
  • Foot support: inner foot rows land on the verified X2D top; outer foot rows land fully on shelves; every foot bottom remains at the same Z=0 datum.
  • Tie clearance: both 3 mm truss spans stay between the measured foot rows and below the real AMS underside; center bosses remain in the middle gap.
  • Door/service: cycle the door fully, check screen and rear access, verify top-glass service strategy, and confirm no part blocks vents, purge hardware, PTFE tubes, cables, or AMS lids.
  • Fasteners: all 34 screws are engaged with nuts; the 16 bracket screw heads sit proud on the shelves, while tie screw heads remain seated in their recesses. No screw may protrude into a keep-out or foot-support zone.
Before operational use: remove the mount and proof-test the printed modules/joins away from the printer with a distributed dead load and sustained warm-load period. Inspect ASA parts for whitening, cracking, permanent set, loose nuts, and layer separation. Add one independent retention tether per AMS.
Top-glass service requires removal or loosening of the low ties after the AMS units are removed. Never attempt to lift the glass through the installed tie pair.

Design status: geometry-validated fit-review prototype. This guide documents the modeled assembly; physical interface verification remains mandatory.
